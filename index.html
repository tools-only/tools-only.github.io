I am interested in AI agents, Agent harness, self-evolution, and systems (with two years of industry experience in systems development). I am particularly curious about how agents learn from experience, adapt their own execution environments, and collaborate with people over long-horizon tasks.

Selected Projects

Agent Self-Evolution Research

2026.07 – Present

Research on Agent-Native Meta-Harness, enabling agents to construct, continuously optimize, and reuse harnesses across tasks.

  • Self-Harness: Architecture design for automatic harness generation, evaluation-driven correction, version management, and cross-task migration.
  • Sandbox & orchestration: Containerized execution and SRT permission constraints for isolated instances, parallel exploration, trajectory collection, and reproducible experiments, including ARC-AGI-3.
  • Agentic RL: Inference deployment and optimization with vLLM / Ascend vLLM; a loop from interactive trajectories to training signals, post-training, and evaluation, exploring GRPO and OPD.

Loom

2026.05 – Present

Independent developer

A human–agent collaboration platform for long-horizon tasks and continuity across sessions.

[Demo]

  • Task-level collaboration: A global task graph connects requirements, plans, progress, and human decisions with local work contexts.
  • Artifact interaction: Structured anchors connect generated artifacts to task context for feedback, local edits, and provenance tracking.
  • Persistent collaboration state: Records agent execution, human interactions, and artifact evolution; Skills and Adapters support different runtimes, state recovery, and human takeover.
