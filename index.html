About Me

I build intelligent systems that can reason, adapt, and collaborate over long horizons.

I am a PhD candidate at Zhejiang University. My current work focuses on agent-native infrastructure, including self-evolving harnesses, sandbox orchestration, and agentic reinforcement learning. I am interested in how agents can move beyond executing tasks toward constructing and improving the systems they use to work.

My broader research spans large language model safety, multimodal perception, interpretability, and optimization. Before my doctoral studies, I worked on operating-system kernels and low-power technologies for connected devices.

2026 — Present
MULTI-AGENT COLLABORATION

Loom

Independent Developer

A collaboration system for long-horizon tasks, centered on task organization, artifact interaction, and durable collaboration state.

  • Links requirements, plans, execution progress, and decisions in a shared task graph.
  • Makes generated artifacts traceable, addressable, and continuously editable through task context.
  • Records agent events and human interactions for state recovery, handoff, and runtime adaptation.
